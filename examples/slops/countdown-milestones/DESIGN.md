# A date to remember

Single job: anticipate one meaningful date and track the steps along the way.

A playful Paper object: powder blue, a pale ice calendar face, two coral binding
tabs, a yellow unit label, and bundled Fredoka numerals. The calendar edge adds
depth; floating circles, mission language, divider lines, and arbitrary elapsed
percentages are gone. Milestone completion is the only progress indicator.

At 440 × 580 the ordinary three-step countdown fits. At 360px the time units stay
visible. Long titles, large numbers, and longer milestone lists expand naturally
and scroll; export includes the complete content. The icon uses the same binding,
number, and unit treatment and distinguishes arrival from elapsed days.

Before the event, the primary unit moves from days to hours to minutes. At the
target time, it becomes Today. After that local calendar day, it counts calendar
days since. Invalid dates show a repairable Choose a date state. Time uses the
local timezone; nonexistent DST times are rejected on Save. Opening, background
recovery, and static export all use the shared countdown calculation.

Date editing uses a Bits UI dialog and calendar popover with a separate draft.
Cancel never writes. Milestones retain their IDs and unknown properties while
allowing direct title editing, independent checking, and explicit reordering.
The existing 25-step limit is visible. The original schema and createdAt field
remain compatible. Completion uses a brief press transition; there are no idle
or automatic arrival animations to settle during capture.
