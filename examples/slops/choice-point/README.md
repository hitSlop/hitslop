# Choice Point

A quiet ACT worksheet based on Dr. Russ Harris’s fork-in-the-road diagram
(*The Happiness Trap*, Choice Point 2.0, after Bailey, Ciarrochi, Harris 2013).

The page is the diagram:

- **Hooks** and **values / helpers** sit above the fork.
- **Away** and **towards** are the two roads — things you do, not things you feel.
- **Challenging situation** is the stem: what showed up.
- **STOP** (Slow down, Take note, Open up, Pursue values) is the pause.
- **One tiny towards move** is the next action, with a time and a done mark.

A short first-launch walkthrough teaches the fill order. Direct editing persists
as one JSON object. Static capture hides add/remove and the tutorial; the icon
is a cream sheet with the fork.
