# Mood Log

A gentle color journal for recording daily mood, energy, and a short reflection without pressure.

- **Job**: Check in with yourself daily, rating mood and energy on a warm chromatic scale with an unhurried one-line reflection.
- **Storage**: Canonical `stores/data.json` storing prompt question, active draft state, and chronological dated check-in entries.
- **Visuals**: Soft linen journal card (`#fbf9f5`), luminous gradient mood orbs, energy dot indicators, and generous breathing room.
