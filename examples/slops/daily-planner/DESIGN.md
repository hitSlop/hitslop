# Daily Planner — a slim day board

Job: make room for what matters today, and see what comes next.

An Instrument: 400 × 900, cool white hour rail, deep teal header and apricot date
and Now control. Mint, teal, apricot, and lavender raised blocks distinguish
labeled categories. The host window is the outer boundary. Typography is Avenir
Next with a tabular monospaced ruler; no cream paper or simulated book fold.

The timeline stays at 40px per hour from 6am to midnight. Priorities and date
controls stay visible, notes open into a roomy dialog, and excess hours scroll.
Today opens with now near the upper third, bounded by the beginning/end of day.
Manual scrolling or editing pauses following; Now returns to today and resumes.
Other dates start near 8am. No automatic movement animates or fights the user.

Draw, move, and resize in 15-minute increments. Gestures remain temporary until
release, cancel with Escape/pointer cancellation, and autoscroll at the edges.
Arrow keys move blocks or resize focused handles. Click/Enter opens a Bits UI
editor. Short titles truncate with full accessible names and dialog details.

Existing schema and unknown fields survive. This is one day's document, not a
calendar archive. Date changes relabel its schedule. Notes use Save/Cancel.
Export uses a chronological list so overlapping/short blocks never truncate
content. The icon is a slim rail with three raised blocks and the now line.

Review: typical, overlapping/long-content, and empty fixtures at 400 and 360px;
editor, full export, and 512px icon. Schedule tests cover boundaries, occupied
minutes, positioning, and adjustment. Routine delivery ends at build/validate.
