# Ambient Sound Mixer

A smoked-glass hi-fi ambient sound mixer with physical faders, VU level meters, presets, and synthesized audio.

- **Job**: Mix, mute, solo, and balance five natural ambient channels (Rain, Thunder, Wind, Birds, Night) with physical faders and save presets.
- **Storage**: Canonical `stores/data.json` storing current preset name, master volume, playing state, channel levels, and custom presets.
- **Visuals**: Smoked-glass hi-fi chassis (`#15171e`), brushed metal fader tracks, green LED VU meter bars (`#22c55e`), and physical fader caps.
