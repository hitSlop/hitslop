# Eisenhower Matrix

An urgent-versus-important priority desk blotter.

- **Quadrant I (Do First)**: Urgent & Important (crises, pressing deadlines).
- **Quadrant II (Schedule)**: Not Urgent & Important (deep work, strategy, exercise, relationships).
- **Quadrant III (Delegate)**: Urgent & Not Important (interruptions, routine requests).
- **Quadrant IV (Don’t Do)**: Not Urgent & Not Important (time sinks, busywork to eliminate).
- **Triage Holding Pen**: Quick capture inbox to park new tasks before sorting them into I, II, III, or IV.
- **Q2 Leverage Metric**: Live ratio of proactive Q2 tasks against total active load.
