# Contact Card

A translucent mint glass badge and digital business card with direct links, bio, and vCard export.

- **Job**: Present personal identity, profile photo, verified contact channels, and instant vCard export in an elegant tactile glass card.
- **Storage**: Canonical `stores/data.json` storing contact details, links, and bio; `stores/media/` for user-chosen avatar photo.
- **Visuals**: Translucent mint glass shell (`rgba(224, 244, 238, 0.88)`), frosted glass bevels, dark forest text (`#143527`), tactile circular icon buttons, and mint status badge.
