# Assignment Tracker · Study pad

Purpose: know what to work on next, and feel the relief of finishing it.

A deep plum header anchors a crisp white pad. Lilac organizes courses and controls; coral signals urgent deadlines; mint celebrates completion. No yellow paper, clip ornament, or tiny worksheet icon. The icon is a bold white sheet and mint checkmark on plum.

Assignment titles lead each row. Course, category, and points are supporting details. Dates and completion stay directly actionable; the row opens a focused editor with staged changes. Notes preview on the list and expand in the editor and export.

At 620 × 700, scan the day's work and then scroll the list. At narrow widths, deadline controls move below the title. Long titles wrap. Export stays in normal document flow with full notes. Reduced-motion preferences disable row movement.

Keep urgency meaningful: overdue, today, next seven days, later, needs a date. Invalid legacy dates remain preserved until explicitly corrected. The palette and component rules live in theme.ts and src/styles.css.ts.
