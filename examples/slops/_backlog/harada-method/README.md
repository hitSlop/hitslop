# Harada Method

The Open Window 64 chart — the mandal-art sheet Shohei Ohtani filled in at sixteen. One core
goal sits at the centre of eighty-one hand-ruled cells. The eight cells around it are the
themes, and each theme opens its own 3×3 block of eight concrete actions.

Editing a theme beside the goal rewrites the centre of its outer block, and the reverse, so
the sheet stays consistent however you work across it. Every action ticks off, and the tally
counts the sixty-four.

Scope is the chart only — not the long-term goal form, the routine sheet, or the diary.
