# School Schedule — your week, in color

Job: know where to be next and give every class and after-class plan a place.
Audience: middle school, high school, and college students.

A bright Instrument with a campus-notebook personality: cobalt header, cool white
board, raised mint/sky/coral/lilac/aqua subject tiles, rounded Outfit lettering,
and a single star tab. The host is the outer boundary; no beige paper, binder
holes, fake window, or official-form language. Colors remain owner-selected and
retain the existing seven stored keys with new display names and token values.

880 × 640 by default. Five days fit across; narrow windows scroll the same board
with sticky weekdays and a sticky bell-time rail. More periods and activities
scroll vertically. Subject and room lead, with full teacher details in a dialog.
The live readout identifies the current or next stop, room, and timing. No idle
pulsing or fake progress. Today and Now labels reinforce color.

Class, period, activity, and student editing use draft Save/Cancel dialogs.
Class destinations can be changed by pointer drag or dialog day/period controls.
Occupied destinations swap; the pointer preview and dialog notice announce that
before commit. Escape and pointer cancellation discard gestures. Deleting a
populated period and copying over other days require explicit confirmation.
Bell periods cannot overlap; existing invalid times are ignored by live status.

Keep the single repeating Monday–Friday schema and unknown fields. Student/term,
homeroom and masked locker details have their own dialog. Export preserves the
existing inclusion of locker details, and expands every class, teacher, room,
and activity in full. A mini board and star form the icon.

Review fixtures: full week, long names/busy afternoon, partial week, fresh start.
Tests cover move/swap, copy, bell validation, and now/next boundaries. Routine
finish is browser review, Svelte checks, build, and package validation.
