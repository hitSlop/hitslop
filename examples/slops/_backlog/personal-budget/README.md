# Personal Budget

A graphite pocket calculator fused with a dark paper ledger for monthly budgeting, spending categories, and quick calculations.

- **Job**: Track monthly income, expense categories, and remaining unallocated budget while providing a tactile physical calculator to crunch numbers and log expenses directly.
- **Storage**: Canonical `stores/data.json` storing current month label, total income, savings target, and categorized expense items (allocated vs spent).
- **Visuals**: Tactile graphite chassis (`#1c1d22`), recessed LCD readout window, safety-orange calculation key (`#ea580c`), high-contrast mint green balances (`#4ade80`), and tabular typography.
