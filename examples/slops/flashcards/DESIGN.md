# Flashcards — bright study deck

Single job: recall a question, reveal its answer, and decide whether to practice
it again. An electric-blue study instrument with white rounded cards replaces
the wooden filing cabinet. Outfit supplies friendly, large study typography;
Avenir supplies controls. The answer side is pale blue, Again is soft coral,
and Got it is mint. There are no wood, brass, yellow-paper, or ruled textures.

Preserve the 540 × 440 window, editable decks/cards, shuffle, four learning
stages, review timestamps, and existing store schema. Retain theme token names
for compatibility even where old material names now map to flat colors.
The card keeps its 3D flip; reduced motion and static capture settle instantly.

One primary action appears below the question: Reveal answer. Once revealed,
Again and Got it replace it. Grading before revealing does nothing. Grading
within a filtered stage follows the next previously visible card rather than
skipping after removal from that stage. New decks start on All. Shortcuts are
Space, 1, and 2; native controls keep Space and form inputs keep typing keys.
Screen readers receive only the active face's content.

At narrow widths all five stage tabs and edit/shuffle/add/delete controls stay
available. Short windows scroll; long card text scrolls inside the face rather
than clipping. Export shows the chosen face fully, without editing controls.
The icon is a question-card stack with a mint checkmark, without tiny fake text.

Review fixtures: question, answer, empty, long answer. Builds ship no fixtures
or authored guidance. The bundled Outfit font carries its OFL license.
