# Morning Pages

Julia Cameron’s three-page stream-of-consciousness brain clearing (*The Artist’s Way*).

- **750-Word Target**: 3 pages of longhand writing (~250 words each).
- **Live Word Odometer**: Continuous word count tracking with a 3-page milestone bar.
- **Distraction-Free Canvas**: Vintage legal pad margin line and generous serif typesetting.
- **Date Archive**: Date navigator to review past entries or jump to today.

## Development

```sh
bun slop dev examples/slops/morning-pages
bun slop build examples/slops/morning-pages
```
